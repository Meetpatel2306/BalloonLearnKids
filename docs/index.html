About the app

Bubble Learn Kids is a calm, cheerful balloon-popping game for toddlers and preschoolers. Tap friendly balloons to pop them into confetti and gently learn letters, numbers, colours, shapes and animals along the way.

  • 🔤 A–Z — hear “A for Apple” with a picture
  • 🔢 1–20 — find each number and watch it fly into place
  • 🌈 Colors — find the balloon of the spoken colour
  • ⭐ Shapes — circle, square, triangle, star and heart
  • 🐶 Animals — fourteen animals and the sounds they make

Safe by design

  • Works 100% offline — the app has no internet permission at all
  • No advertising, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions
  • No data collected and nothing sent anywhere
  • No score pressure, no timers, nothing a child can get wrong
  • Settings are protected so a child cannot change them by accident
