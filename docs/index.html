How a round works

Simple enough for a two‑year‑old

  1. The app asks for one thing. “Find C” is shown on a card and said out loud.
  2. The right balloon glows. If your child waits a moment, a gentle hint appears — never a scolding.
  3. They tap it and it pops. The letter is spoken again, then a word: “C for Cat”.
  4. The next target arrives. Letters run A to Z in order, so there is a real beginning and end.
  5. Finishing is a party. Confetti, sounds and stars — then straight back to the menu.

Designed for kids & families

Small details that matter at this age

Nothing to get wrong

No timer, no losing, no game over. A tap on empty sky makes a friendly sparkle, never a buzzer.

Safe by construction

The app has no internet permission at all, so it cannot send anything anywhere — not even by accident.

Settings kept for grown-ups

A small sum has to be answered before Settings will open, so little fingers cannot change things by mistake.

Everything spoken aloud

Each letter, number, colour and shape is said clearly, so a child who cannot read yet can still play alone.

Small download, no extras

Everything arrives in the single install. There is no second download and no account to create.

Adjustable pace

Balloon speed, size, sound, background sound, vibration and high contrast can all be tuned by a grown-up.

A note for parents

Balloon Learn Kids was built to be the kind of app a parent can hand over without checking first. It collects no information whatsoever, shows no advertising, contains no in‑app purchases and has no links that open a browser or take a child out of the app.

The first time it is opened, a short notice explains all of this and has to be acknowledged before play begins. After that the app never asks for anything again. Full details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
