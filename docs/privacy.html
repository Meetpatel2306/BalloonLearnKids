Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

In short: Bubble Learn Kids collects no personal information, sends nothing over the internet, and shows no advertising. It works entirely on your device.

Who we are

Bubble Learn Kids is developed by Meet Patel. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at bubblelearnkids@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for a name, an email address, an age, a photograph, a contact or a location. There is no sign-up and no user account. We do not use cookies or advertising identifiers.

Information we send

None. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it is technically incapable of sending data to us or to anyone else. The only permission the app requests is permission to vibrate, used for the small buzz when a balloon pops.

Third-party services

The app contains no advertising network, no analytics service, no crash reporting service and no social media integration.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of information in its own private storage so it can remember your preferences: whether sound, background sounds and vibration are on, the chosen balloon speed and size, whether high contrast is on, whether the introductory hint has been shown, and a short list of recent scores for each game. This never leaves the device and is removed when the app is uninstalled. The app also writes small generated sound files to its temporary cache folder, which Android may delete at any time.

Children

The app is designed for children aged approximately two to five, and is intended to be used with a parent or carer nearby. Because it collects and transmits no information at all, it does not knowingly process personal information from children under 13 as defined by the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), nor personal data of any individual under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Purchases and external links

The app contains no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no advertising. It contains no links that open a web browser or take a child outside the app.

Your rights

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. To remove everything the app has stored on your device, uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the date above before the new version is released.
