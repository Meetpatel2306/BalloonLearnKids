# Privacy Policy — Bubble Learn

_Last updated: 5 August 2026_

## Short version

Bubble Learn collects nothing, sends nothing and stores nothing about you or your
child. It has no internet access.

## The long version

**Data we collect: none.** The app does not ask for a name, an email address, an
age, a photo, a contact, a location or an account. There is no sign-up and no
profile.

**Data we transmit: none.** The app does not request the Android `INTERNET`
permission. It is technically incapable of sending data anywhere, and you can
verify this yourself in `app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml` — the only permission
requested in the entire app is `VIBRATE`, used for the small buzz when a bubble
pops.

**Third-party code: none.** There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no
crash reporter and no social network integration. The app's only dependencies
are Google's own AndroidX and Jetpack Compose UI libraries.

**Data stored on the device.** Two settings — whether sound is on and whether
vibration is on — are saved in the app's private storage. They are removed when
the app is uninstalled. A few small generated sound files are written to the
app's cache folder on first launch; Android may delete them at any time.

**Purchases and links.** There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no
links that leave the app.

**Children.** The app is designed for children aged roughly 2 to 5. Because it
collects and transmits no data at all, it does not process personal information
of children under 13 as defined by COPPA, nor personal data of any data subject
under the GDPR.

**Changes.** If this ever changes, it will be recorded in this file and in the
project's release notes before the change ships.

**Contact.** Open an issue on the project's repository.
